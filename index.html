About

Practice should transfer.

The problem

The range lets you erase mistakes.

Raking over the same club and immediately correcting the last swing can make practice look better without making golf easier.

The method

One ball. One decision. One consequence.

Range Lab gives you a club, a personal distance, and an intention. Pick a target, commit, hit once, and accept the result.

Two modes

Random questions or course-like sequences.

Random Practice moves through the bag without immediate repeats. Play the Course links tee shots, approaches, and scoring shots into believable holes.

Honest analytics

A common miss is not automatically a tendency.

Range Lab shows counts and percentages. It only calls something a pattern when enough recorded shots support it.

Privacy

Your golf data stays with you.

Sessions are stored on this device. There are no accounts, trackers, ads, or outside data calls. Use CSV export to keep a backup.
